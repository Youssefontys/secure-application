Privacy statement

Who am I?

I am youssefontys, a student, and I build this app for an assignment. I am responsible for the data you enter here. Questions or requests: this@maildoestnotexit.com.

What data do I keep?

  • Your username, and your password as a hash: the password itself is stored nowhere.
  • If you turn on two-step verification: the secret of your authenticator app (encrypted) and a hash of your recovery code.
  • Your notes: title and text, encrypted, with the time they were created and changed.
  • A security log: time, kind of event (for example "login failed"), your IP address and, if your account exists, your username. Passwords and codes are never in it.
  • A session cookie to keep you logged in, for 8 hours at most. I use no other cookies and no tracking.

I do not ask for an email address, a name or a phone number. In your notes you can type anything yourself. Please do not put sensitive data in them, such as health data: this is a test environment.

Why, and on what ground?

  • I keep your account and notes to make the app work, with your consent: the box you tick when you register. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.
  • I keep the security log to see and stop attacks, because of a legitimate interest. That interest comes first because the log is worthless otherwise, so it does not depend on your consent.
  • I use your data only for this. No marketing, and I share it with no one other than listed below.

Where, and who has it?

  • On Microsoft Azure in France Central, in the European Union. Microsoft processes the data on my behalf (processor).
  • No one else gets your data. The code is public on GitHub, but without the data of users.
  • When an address is blocked (too many failed attempts), an administrator gets a message by email.

The hacking week

In the hacking week, fellow students attack the app, with permission. As a result, data in the environment may come into the hands of people who normally do not see it. So do not use real data in that week.

What are your rights?

You can view your profile and notes, change notes, and delete your account with all its notes. The app has no export of your notes yet; ask for one at this@maildoestnotexit.com if you want them. I answer a request within a month.

A complaint?

If we cannot work it out, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).
